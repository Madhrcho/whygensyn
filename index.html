The Network for Machine Intelligence

What is Gensyn?

Gensyn is a decentralized network designed to foster machine intelligence alongside human intelligence. It connects devices worldwide into an open, permissionless, and neutral system for AI development. Inspired by nature, it enables AI to compress and share knowledge without gatekeepers, making it scalable to every computer globally.

Why is Gensyn Needed?

  • Privacy Risks: Your data often goes to external companies.
  • High Costs: Training AI requires massive hardware.
  • Limited Access: Only a few control AI.
  • Scalability Challenges: Global AI scaling is hard.

Benefits for Users

  • Everyday Users: Train AI locally with BlockAssist.
  • Developers: Integrate AI into apps collaboratively.
  • ML Researchers: Deploy swarms and explore decentralized AI.
  • All Users: Enhanced privacy, lower costs, fairer AI.

How Does It Work?

  • RL Swarm: Collaborative AI training over the internet.
  • Judge & Verde: Trustless verification ensuring reproducibility.
  • Communication Tools: NoLoCo & SkipPipe for distributed training.
